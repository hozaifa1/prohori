# Capture protocol: first photo set (D03)

About 45 minutes. Phone camera only, no special gear.

## Before you go
1. Print `data/print/PRINT_ME_charuco_A4.pdf` at **100% / Actual size** (turn off "fit to page").
2. Measure the 100 mm bar with a ruler. Write the number on the sheet. If it's off by more than 1 mm, reprint.
3. Cut out the board with a small white margin and glue it **flat** onto stiff cardboard. Matte paper, no lamination.

## Per crack (aim for 15–20 cracks)
Walls, columns, stair landings, boundary walls, plastered or bare, painted or not. Hairline cracks count too.

1. Hold or tape the board **flat against the wall, right beside the crack** (within ~5 cm), same surface plane. Don't cover the crack.
2. Take **5 photos**:
   - A. Straight-on, close: phone ~20–25 cm away, board and crack both fully in frame.
   - B. Straight-on, farther: ~50 cm.
   - C. Tilted ~30° to the left or right.
   - D. Deliberately bad: slightly blurry (move while shooting) **or** strong glare on the board.
   - E. Straight-on, close, a second time (repeatability).
3. Default camera app, no zoom, no portrait mode, no filters. Flash off unless it's dark.
4. Optional but valuable: if you have a crack-width card or feeler gauge, note your reading in a text file.

## Hand-off
Put everything, unrenamed, in `F:/Projects/Prohori/data/raw/2026-09-19/`. Add `notes.txt` with one line per crack if you like (location, surface type, e.g. "stair wall, painted plaster").

Phone photos keep their EXIF data (camera model, focal length), which I use. Don't send them through WhatsApp or Messenger, because that strips it. Copy by USB or Google Drive instead.

Faces: avoid people in frame. The pipeline redacts faces anyway.
