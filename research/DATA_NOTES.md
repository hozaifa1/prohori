## 2. Synthetic photoreal generator (`src/prohori/synth.py`)

### 2.1 Why it exists and what it can and cannot support

* **METRICS 1a** names "synthetic renders (known exact widths, controlled blur, noise and perspective)" as the remaining ground truth when the printed panel and krkCMd (M1) cannot supply it; **C2** (LITERATURE 7.4) needs "synthetic photoreal cracks with a varying interior contrast". Field capture is impossible here (CAPTURE_PROTOCOL dropped it), so every render carries exact per-station crack width in mm and the exact camera.
* **A3 (CRC) caveat:** the conformal / risk-control guarantees hold only for captures exchangeable with the calibration set. Synthetic renders are *not* exchangeable with real phone photos, so **they must not be used to claim a certified-but-wrong rate or interval coverage for real captures** (METRICS section 2 caveat). They are for estimator development (C1, C2, C4), stress tests (METRICS section 6) and gate-feature design (C3), and the panel stays the calibration source.

### 2.3 Known sim-to-real gaps (stated so no result is over-read)

1. The real photos are of a printed sheet on a desk; the renders are a printed sheet on a textured wall. Only the board region is statistically comparable, which is why Part A of `synth_validation.md` compares rectified-board statistics.
2. Texture is one photographic 2K tile, extended below its native resolution with generic power-law grain: no real micro-relief, no aggregate shadowing, no raking light, no paint flaking at the crack edge, no efflorescence.
3. Crack appearance is a darkness attenuation with soft width-dependent depth; real cracks have chipped edges, interior shadows that depend on light direction and depth, and bright rims.
4. Colour processing (demosaic, tone curve, sharpening, denoise) is a generic ISP model, not a specific phone.
5. Texture "false cracks" in the renders come from pores and mottling; real walls also have joints, paint edges and stains (S6) that are only partially represented.
6. Real-photo statistics are from 29 photos of one phone: distribution comparisons carry sampling error (KS D below about 0.35 cannot be distinguished from noise).
