# PROHORI — Literature review, 2024–2026

*Compiled 2026-09-18. Scope: crack segmentation, crack-width metrology, active perception / agentic inspection quality, and benchmark metrics. Companion file: [METRICS.md](METRICS.md).*

## How this review was built

**Inclusion rules**

1. **Published 2024–2026.** Nothing earlier is cited as evidence. Older work shows up only where a 2024+ paper builds on it, for example the clIoU metric that OmniCrack30k adopted.
2. **Vetted venue.** The paper must be accepted at a peer-reviewed venue with real selectivity: CVPR / ICCV / ECCV / WACV / ICLR / ICML / ICRA / CoRL / ACM MM / MICCAI and their official workshops, or the journals *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering* (CACAIE), *Automation in Construction*, *Advanced Engineering Informatics*, *Engineering Structures*, *Construction and Building Materials*, *ASCE J. Computing in Civil Engineering*, *J. Civil Structural Health Monitoring*, and Nature-portfolio journals. arXiv was used **only as the place to get the full text** of papers accepted elsewhere. Preprints with no acceptance are listed in §6 as excluded, and no finding here depends on them.
3. **Full text, not abstracts.** Every paper in §1–§4 was downloaded and its method, loss, metric definitions and results tables were read. Six relevant papers are paywalled with no open-access copy. They are kept in a separate list (§5), marked *abstract-level*, and **none of them is used to set a target**.
4. **Venue checked, not assumed.** Acceptance was checked against the arXiv `journal_ref`/comments, Crossref DOIs, or the conference's open-access proceedings.

**Read-level key:** **FT** = full text read (method, equations, tables). **AB** = abstract or publisher metadata only.

| M1 | Jakubowski, J., Tomczak, K. **Dataset for developing deep learning models to assess crack width and self-healing progress in concrete (krkCMd).** DOI [10.1038/s41597-025-04485-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04485-z) · data: Zenodo [10.5281/zenodo.11408398](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11408398) | **Scientific Data 2025** · FT | Flatbed CCD scans at 6400 dpi, i.e. **3.97 µm/px (≈252 px/mm)**. **19,098 grey-level profiles** (501 px each) sampled on grid lines *across* cracks, each paired with an operator reference width taken at the maximum brightness gradient. | M2 | Hassouna, M., Marzouk, M., Fathalla, E. **Automated low-cost framework for crack measurements in RC structures using deep learning approach.** [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13156299/) · DOI [10.1038/s41598-026-50880-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-50880-w) | **Scientific Reports 2026** · FT | **This is almost exactly Prohori's pipeline, published:** a 12 MP smartphone about 1.0 m from the wall (field of view ≈400 × 300 mm, so **≈10 px/mm**). | M3 | Deng et al. (CACAIE 2026) — **see S8** | CACAIE 2026 · FT | 3D width from the SLAM point cloud: skeleton → left and right edge points → Euclidean distance. | MAE 0.09 mm, MRE 14.37%, n = 15, against a microscope. | M4 | Liu, H. et al. SCSegamba **supplementary** · and Ge et al. | M5 | *(derivation, not a citation)* **Area conservation under blur.** | — | For a dark bar of true width *w* and contrast *C* against background *b*, any normalised point-spread function preserves the integral of the deficit: `∫ (b − I(s)) ds = C·w`, whatever the blur. | Our own synthetic run (ADDENDUM) found area/length error below 0.4 px for widths ≤ 9 px, against a constant +1.00 px for `2·distanceTransform`. | **The core of Prohori's sub-pixel width estimator (§8).** It needs an estimate of *C*. On the test panel, *C* comes from the ChArUco black squares, the same toner under the same light. On real concrete it comes from the widest local section of the same crack. The published M1 profiles let us validate this before touching real walls. |

**Why marker-based scale is sufficient, and where it breaks.** M2 uses the same rectification strategy as Prohori and still reaches only ≈5.8% COV on wide cracks. The residual there comes from segmentation and edge placement, not from the marker, which is consistent with S10. Scale error from the board is second order provided the board is **coplanar with the crack**. That assumption is violated on corners, columns and uneven plaster, and in that case the agent must `reposition_marker` rather than measure.

## 7. Architectural synthesis — the recommended Prohori pipeline

### 7.1 The five findings that decide the architecture

1. **DNN mask edges are not metrology-grade.** In OmniCrack30k (S10), the best model matches the human centreline to the exact pixel 16% of the time, and to within 1 px only 38%. Dataset labels themselves are not accurate to one pixel. A width taken from a network's binary boundary therefore inherits label noise of ±1 px per edge, about ±0.13 mm at 15 px/mm, which is exactly the error budget in the locked concept. → **Segmentation *locates*, grey-level profiles *measure*.**
2. **Published phone pipelines under-read thin cracks by about 11% with about 22% spread** (M2, at ≈10 px/mm), and even LiDAR rigs show 15–34% relative error below 0.5 mm (S8). → **Resolution gate plus closed-form bias correction plus an N-frame spread** is the right response, and the headline claim is "better than M2 below 0.5 mm, on instrumented ground truth".
3. **The accuracy frontier is ~1–3 M-param Mamba/RWKV models (S1, S4), but they cannot be loaded by `cv2.dnn`.** EfficientCrackNet (S2, 0.26 M params, 0.48 GFLOPs, ONNX-friendly) is the deployable DNN, and SAM-class models are offline teachers (S6, S8). → **Runtime stays OpenCV-native on Graviton**, consistent with the COOL rubric.
4. **Foundation-model agents are weak at low-level quality perception (A1) and at small visual subjects (A2).** → **The agent reasons over numeric CV observations, not over pixels.** This also keeps the P0/P1/P2 ablation clean.
5. **Distribution-free calibration exists and is cheap (A3, A5).** → **The refusal gate and the width interval are conformally calibrated**, which turns "declines to certify" from a heuristic into a stated statistical guarantee. As far as this review found, no 2024–2026 crack-width paper does this. *That is the single most defensible novelty claim available to Prohori.*

### 7.2 Pipeline (each stage → OpenCV call → evidence)

```
PHONE (HTTPS PWA, getUserMedia)
  │  frames @ ~2–5 fps upload, full-res stills on demand
  ▼
[1] OBSERVE — deterministic quality vector q            (A1, A2: never ask the LLM to judge pixels)
    CharucoDetector.detectBoard → #corners, reprojection RMS
    solvePnP → obliquity θ, distance
    homography scale → px/mm at crack ROI
    Laplacian variance (blur), saturated-pixel fraction (glare)
  │
  ▼
[2] DECIDE — gate λ̂ calibrated by Conformal Risk Control  (A3)
    q ≥ λ̂ ? → go to [3]
    else → triage agent (Bedrock) gets q as JSON and picks 1 of 6 actions
           + writes the instruction                      (A6, A7: information-seeking policy)
           → human re-shoots → back to [1]
  │
  ▼
[3] RECTIFY — warpPerspective to a fronto-parallel plane at fixed S px/mm
  │
  ▼
[4] LOCATE (not measure) — crack ROI + centreline
    primary:  CLAHE → adaptiveThreshold → morphology → ximgproc.thinning
    ablation: EfficientCrackNet-class ONNX via cv2.dnn   (S2)
    QA gate:  reject mask if EDT-max blow-up > 2× or holes > 0  (S8)
  │
  ▼
[5] MEASURE — sub-pixel grey-level profile width at K stations along the skeleton
    tangent from skeleton PCA → normal n
    cv2.remap samples I(s) along n (±3w)
    w_area = ∫(b − I) ds / C                               (M5; Equal-Area prior art, CACAIE 2025)
    C from the ChArUco black squares (panel) / widest local section (field)
    cross-check: 2·DT − 1.0 px (closed-form bias, ADDENDUM)
  │
  ▼
[6] AGGREGATE — median over stations, then over N rectified frames
    empirical spread across frames = Type-A uncertainty
  │
  ▼
[7] CALIBRATE — conformal width interval                  (A5 + split-conformal residuals)
    [ŵ − r̂_lo, ŵ + r̂_hi] at 90%, r̂ from panel calibration residuals
  │
  ▼
[8] REPORT — width, interval, px/mm, θ, exposure class + ACI reference value,
    trend vs last visit (ALIKED/DISK + LightGlue re-ID), "not a safety verdict"
```

### 7.3 What the segmentation literature says *not* to do

- **Do not** put a Mamba or SAM model on the live path. Neither `cv2.dnn` nor the COOL story supports it, and S10 shows it would not improve width anyway.
- **Do not** report 2-class mIoU as "accuracy". Report crack-class IoU, F1 and clIoU_ρ (S7, S10).
- **Do not** expect topology losses to move width error (S9: +0.3 Dice on cracks).
- **Do** keep a DNN in the ablation, because judges will ask. EfficientCrackNet-class on `cv2.dnn`, evaluated on the *same* rectified ROIs as the classical path and scored on **width error**, not only IoU. If it helps, it is because it locates cracks better in clutter (TUT, Facade390), and that is where to test it.
