## How the concept was chosen

Seven candidates → three were built out in full by independent agents → three harsh critics judged them blind against **FREISA**, the 2023 Grand Prize winner → that bar proved too easy (FREISA is non-compliant with the 2026 rules: no OpenCV, no cloud), so the bar was raised to **ARGUS**, a red-team entry representing a funded five-person team → a final Opus review audited every load-bearing claim.

Corrections that survived into the locked concept:

- **ACI 224R-01 Table 4.1** was misquoted; more importantly it is *durability and appearance* guidance, **not a structural-capacity criterion**. The system therefore never emits a safety verdict.
- **SDNET2018 has no pixel masks** — it is image-level classification. Segmentation moved to CrackForest / Crack500 / DeepCrack.
- **The cross-generation Graviton2/3/4 COOL study was withdrawn as invalid**: the three Marketplace listings ship different COOL versions built on different OpenCV versions, so the chart would confound microarchitecture with library version.
- **Multi-view triangulation was cut.** On a locally planar surface a ChArUco board plus `solvePnP` gives pose and absolute scale directly, better conditioned and far cheaper to build.
- **Autonomous redeploy was cut** — it scores worse on human control and responsible operation than it gains on autonomy.
- **The classical `imgproc` path became the core product**, so COOL genuinely runs the core workload rather than a side benchmark.

## First measurement result (already run)

The crack-width estimator carries an **exactly constant +1.00 px bias**, independent of width — a pixel-centre convention artifact, removable in closed form:

| True width (px) | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 | 15 | 21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2·distanceTransform` at skeleton | 4.00 | 6.00 | 8.00 | 10.00 | 16.00 | 22.00 |
| Error | +1.00 | +1.00 | +1.00 | +1.00 | +1.00 | +1.00 |

Week one is not planning. It is the thinnest end-to-end vertical slice — phone frame → ChArUco → one millimetre number, live on an **HTTPS** endpoint. `getUserMedia` requires a secure context, so TLS is mandatory, not optional; without it the judge's camera will not open at all.
