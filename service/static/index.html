Tape the printed ChArUco board beside the crack and fill the frame. Prohori measures each crack against the board, then tells you what to do next.

Vision
Finds the board, rectifies the plane and measures every crack in millimetres, with an uncertainty.
Gate
Certifies each crack on its own, from scale, sharpness, tilt and glare weighed together, with a 90% range. Flags the ones it cannot vouch for.
Agent
Reads those numbers, never the pixels, and picks the next action: accept, move closer, relight, or abandon.

Agent trace

Vision turns pixels into numbers. The agent sees only those numbers, then chooses the next action and writes the instruction you read.
