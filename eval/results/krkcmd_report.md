## 7. Method summary

- Simulation: sRGB->linear, exposure normalised to 0.75, Gaussian PSF sigma 0.75 px (output px; sensitivity 0.5,1.0), exact box integration at a random sub-pixel phase, Poisson shot noise (full well 4000 e-) + read noise 3 e-, sRGB encode, 8-bit rounding.
- Native blur sigma already in the scans: 19.5 um (median of wide cracks), added in quadrature to the `sigma_mm` handed to the blend.
- Calibration: least squares ref = a*pred + b on the 18 calibration images at native, frozen (`cal_native`), or refit at each resolution (`cal_refit`); metrics on the other half. Bootstrap CIs resample specimen images.
- Ink reference for the ink / blend estimators: scanner black (linear 0), the analogue of the board's printed black.
