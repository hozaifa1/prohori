## 1. Risk definition

* **wrong**: |ŵ − w| > max(0.05 mm, 10 % · w) with w the printed (nominal) width.  0.05 mm is the resolution of the microscope reference in Hassouna et al. (Sci. Rep. 2026) and the tau of METRICS.md; 10 % is tighter than the mean relative error of both published pipelines (12.7 %, Hassouna; 14.4 %, Deng).  The nominal width is the reference (toner spread on the print is not corrected), so errors below ~0.03 mm should not be over-read.  **Width convention:** the panel truth is the full printed stroke width; krkCMd's `MANwidth` is the operator's black-core width, so the krkCMd-derived `u_res` term is used only as a *resolution-added* error (estimator at px/mm vs the same estimator at native), never as an accuracy against the panel truth.
* **certified**: mask QA passes **and** predicted log(|err|/tol) <= theta.
* **risk** (the CRC loss, Angelopoulos et al. 2024): per capture i, L_i(λ) = (1/m_i) Σ_j 1[crack j certified at λ] · 1[crack j wrong], m_i = measured cracks in the capture (a fixed denominator, so L_i is non-increasing in λ = −theta and bounded by B = 1).  Captures with no measured crack have L = 0 and are left out of the calibration set (conservative).
* also reported: pooled joint rate (certified-and-wrong / all measured) and the conditional rate P(wrong | certified) = certified-and-wrong / certified, which is what a user feels.
