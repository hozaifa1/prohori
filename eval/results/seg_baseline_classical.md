# Classical locator: segmentation baseline

Generated by `eval/seg_baseline.py`, 104 min. Crack-class metrics only (METRICS section 0 rule 3); pooled over the test split unless stated.

The classical locator is **not trained**; METRICS 4c sets no target for it on the public sets (it is scored on centreline recall and downstream width error). The numbers are the honest baseline that a DNN locator (EfficientCrackNet-class, S2) has to beat, and they show why mask edges from either path are not the width measurement (S10).

## Setup

- Locator: `prohori.segeval.ClassicalLocator`, a pixel-domain re-implementation of `measure._locate_cracks` (equal masks on a synthetic crack: `tests/test_segeval.py::test_locator_matches_measure_py`). Differences: no board/validity mask; no perspective rectification; BFS longest path by scipy; stage-2 (corridor half-max + profile) not applied except in the `refined` variant, which uses half of each component's 90th-percentile darkness as a stand-in.
- Dataset images have no scale, so the nominal px/mm S is a free parameter: **default S = 5** (middle of measure.py's 3-8 px/mm locator range, threshold 0.12 as in measure.py) and **tuned S\*** chosen on train/val data only (table below). The threshold sweep is 0.06..0.80 (12 values); the `ODS_*` rows use the best dataset-level threshold on the test split itself (an oracle operating point, as in ODS by definition).
- `pipeline_default` rows are the exact operating point measure.py would use (S=5, threshold 0.12).

## Notes and limits

- OmniCrack30k itself is distributed on request only; the `omni_public` row pools its public constituents (CFD, AEL, CrackTree200, GAPs384). See research/DATASETS.md.
- Test sets are deterministic every-n-th subsets of the official test split (`stride` column in the CSV): crack500 /4, tut /2, facade390 /2, road420 /2, cracktree200 /2, gaps384 /4, crackseg9k /8; the rest are complete.
- clIoU uses Zhang-Suen thinning of mask and ground truth (`segeval.skeleton`); ground-truth masks of the datasets are themselves only ~1-2 px accurate (S10), so clIoU_0px is expected to be low for any method.
- Per-image `clIoU_*_mean_img` variants and the full threshold curves are available from `segeval.SweepAccumulator`.

## Reading the numbers (run of 2026-10-19)

- **The pipeline-default locator (S = 5, threshold 0.12) finds nothing on textured, dark surfaces** (crack IoU 0.000 on Crack500, Road420, AEL, GAPs384; 0.03-0.07 on DeepCrack, TUT, CFD, CrackSeg9k). A darkness of 12 % below the local maximum is exceeded by the whole asphalt or concrete texture, the texture merges into one component and the width/length rules drop it. That threshold was set for printed paper and plaster after rectification, not for raw street photos. This is the honest answer to "what does the classical locator do on public data without tuning".
- **Tuned (scale chosen on train/val, threshold chosen per dataset, so an optimistic upper bound):** crack IoU 0.55 on DeepCrack (F1 0.71, against 0.86-0.91 for trained networks), 0.26 on Crack500 (F1 0.41 against 0.755 / 0.791), 0.34 on TUT (F1 0.51 against 0.84), and 0.06-0.24 on the cross-domain sets (Facade390 0.16, Road420 0.11, CFD 0.20, CrackSeg9k 0.24). The classical path is therefore 20-45 F1 points behind the trained models in-domain, as METRICS 4c predicted; it is not a competitive segmenter and is not used as one.
- **Exact-pixel centreline agreement is poor for everything**: clIoU_0px <= 0.23 even on the easiest set, 0.52 at 4 px (DeepCrack). That is the S10 finding (best nnU-Net 0.162 at 0 px on validation): labels and masks agree on the crack but not on the pixel, so widths must come from grey levels. Centreline recall @ 2 px is 0.66 on DeepCrack and 0.29-0.50 on the harder sets, which is the quantity that decides how many width stations survive.
- The `refined` (half-maximum) variant changes nothing material (+-0.01 IoU), so the located component, not the thresholding inside it, is the limit.
